Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to view its answer.

How do I book a ticket?

Choose a movie, event or play, select your date and show, choose seats, and follow the booking steps to confirm.

Where can I find my booking?

Check the confirmation shown after booking and the booking details available in your account, if enabled.

Can I cancel my booking?

Cancellation depends on the event or venue policy. Check the terms shown before booking or contact our support team.

How do I request a refund?

Contact support with your booking ID, registered email address and reason for the request. Refund eligibility is checked against the booking policy.

How long does a refund take?

If approved, the time taken depends on the payment provider and bank. Keep your booking ID for follow-up.

I was charged but did not receive a confirmation. What should I do?

Please do not immediately make another payment. Contact support with the transaction reference and registered email so the payment can be checked.

Which payment methods can I use?

Available payment methods are displayed at checkout. Use only the options shown on the payment screen.

Is online payment secure?

Use the payment page provided during checkout and never share your password, OTP or card PIN with anyone.

Can I change my seat after booking?

Seat changes depend on availability and the venue policy. Contact support as soon as possible with your booking ID.

Can I change the date or showtime?

Changes are subject to availability and the applicable booking policy. Our support team can explain the options for your booking.

I entered the wrong email address. Can it be corrected?

Contact support and provide your booking ID and the correct email address. We may ask for details to verify the booking.

What should I do if my ticket is not loading?

Refresh the page and check your internet connection. If it still does not appear, contact support with your booking ID.

Do children need a ticket?

Age and ticket requirements vary by movie, venue and event. Check the details on the listing before booking.

How can I check the age rating of a movie?

Open the movie details page and review the listed rating and any age-related guidance.

Are food and beverages included in the ticket price?

Unless the listing specifically says so, food and beverages are generally not included in the ticket price.

What time should I arrive at the venue?

Arrive early enough to pass venue entry checks and find your seat. Check your ticket or event listing for venue-specific instructions.

Can I book tickets for a group?

Choose the number of seats allowed at checkout. If you need more seats than the page permits, contact the venue or support team.

I selected the wrong city. What should I do?

Choose the correct city before selecting a show. If you have already booked, contact support because the booking may not be transferable.

Why is a show not appearing?

It may not be available in your selected city or date, or bookings may not have opened. Try changing the filters or checking later.

How do I know if my booking is successful?

Look for the on-screen confirmation and booking reference. If payment was deducted but confirmation is missing, contact support.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Ticket transfer rules vary by venue and event. Check the listing terms or contact support before sharing a ticket.

What if an event is postponed or cancelled?

Follow the updates provided by the organiser and check your registered contact channel. Contact support if you need help with the booking.

I forgot my account password. How can I log in?

Use the available login or password recovery option on the sign-in page. If recovery is unavailable, contact support.

How can I report a technical problem?

Tell us which page you were using, what went wrong, and your booking ID if relevant. A screenshot can also help explain the issue.

How do I contact TheatreX support?

Call the helpline or email support@theatrex.com. Include your booking ID and registered email for booking-related questions.

Still need help?

We’ll help you find the next step. Keep your booking ID handy.
